# Roles and access

Your admin role decides which screens you see in Administration and what you can do on
each one.

## How to Open

**Where you see it:** the **Tenant** selector sits at the top of every Administration screen, next to the notification bell. On a wide screen it is at the top right. On a tablet or phone it sits at the top of the menu drawer, which you open with the menu button at the top left. The access level shows as a small label at the top of each screen (View only, Edit or Approve). A locked screen shows a lock in the menu.

## Groups are what you manage

**Your group:** you manage one or more groups of clients. Everything you see and change
belongs to the group you are working in.

**Switching group:** the group you are working in shows at the top of the screen, labelled
**Tenant**. If you manage more than one, pick another from that list. You stay on the same
screen if you have access to it in the new group.

**Every group:** some admins can work across every group.

## Access levels

You hold one level on each screen or tool, in each group you manage. The level shows as a small label at the top of the screen: **View only**, **Edit** or **Approve**.

**View only:** you see the screen and can export what it shows. You cannot change anything.

**Edit:** you can also make changes. Many changes become a request that another admin
approves.

**Approve:** you can also approve or reject other admins' requests.

## Locked and hidden

**Hidden:** you have no access to a screen in any group, so it is not in the menu.

**Locked:** you have access to a screen in another group but not in this one. It shows a
lock, and opening it tells you which group you do have access in. Switch group to use it.

## Regular users

Your clients need no role. Roles are only for admins. What a client can use in the app is
set with Feature rules.

## When your access changes

A change to your role reaches your app within 15 minutes, or sooner when you reopen the
app.

## Refreshing

Screens never refresh on their own. Each screen shows when it was last updated. Use the
refresh button next to that time to load the latest data.
