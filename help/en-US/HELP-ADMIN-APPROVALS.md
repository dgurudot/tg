# Approvals

Many changes in Administration need two people: one admin asks for the change and a
different admin approves it. Nothing changes for your clients until the request is
approved.

## How to Open

**Where you see it:** your own requests and the ones waiting for you are on the Approvals screen. On a wide screen choose **Approvals** in the side menu. On a tablet or phone open the menu drawer with the menu button at the top left, then choose **Approvals**. Edit screens show "Nothing changes until it is approved" when you send a request. You need at least View on Approvals to see the lists, and Approve to decide a request.

## Why you cannot approve your own request

**Fewer mistakes:** a second person checks every sensitive change before it reaches
clients.

**A clear record:** every request, approval and rejection is kept, with who did it, when
and why.

**No exceptions:** you cannot approve a request you made, whatever your role. Your own
request shows "Waiting for another approver." You can withdraw it instead.

## Asking for a change

**Who can ask:** anyone with Edit on that screen.

**What you give:** the change and a reason. The reason is required.

**What happens next:** you see a message such as "Nothing changes until it is approved."
The request appears on the Approvals screen under **Mine**.

## The Approvals screen

**For me:** requests you can decide, with the ones that expire soonest first. Columns show
the request id, what it changes, the screen it belongs to, who asked, when, and when it
expires.

**Mine:** requests you made and what happened to them.

**Decided:** requests you approved or rejected.

## Approving

Open a request to see what it changes, from the current value to the new one, who asked,
their reason, and how long is left.

**Approve:** you confirm it is you by entering your password again. The change then
applies. You see "Approved. Applying now." and then "Applied."

## Rejecting

**Reject:** choose **Reject…**, give a reason, then **Submit rejection**. The change does
not happen.

## Expiry

A request expires if nobody decides it in time. The time left turns gold with less than
24 hours to go and red with less than 6. Ask again if you still need the change.

## Request states

**Pending:** waiting for a decision.

**Approved:** approved and being applied.

**Applied:** in effect.

**Rejected:** turned down, with the reason.

**Expired:** nobody decided in time.

**Failed:** approved, but it could not be applied. Nothing changed.

**Withdrawn:** the person who asked cancelled it.
