# Clients

Clients is where you find, look after and support the people in your group. It has three
tabs: **Clients**, **Sign-ups** and **Accounts**.

## How to Open

**Wide screen:** choose **Clients** in the side menu on the left. Use the menu button at the top right to collapse the menu to icons or expand it again.

**Tablet and phone:** below 1000 pixels wide the side menu becomes a drawer. Open it with the menu button at the top left, then choose **Clients**.

**Tab:** The screen has three tabs under the title: **Clients**, **Sign-ups** and **Accounts**.

**Access needed:** You need access to Clients, Onboarding & KYC or Accounts. **View** lets you read. **Edit** lets you make requests and add notes. **Approve** lets you approve sign-ups.

**Locked:** if you have access in another group but not the one you are working in, **Clients** shows a lock in the menu. Opening it says "You do not have access to Clients in this tenant" and names the group where you do have access. Switch group with **Tenant** at the top. If you have no access in any group, **Clients** is not in the menu at all.

## Clients tab

**Search:** find a client by name or email.

**Columns:** Status, KYC, Group, Assigned, Accts and Last active show by default. You can
add Country, Risk band and Created. A value that is not tracked yet shows "Not tracked yet".

**Filters:** Status, Mine only, Assigned, Country and Risk band. The KYC and Group filters
are coming soon.

**Open a client:** tap a row to open the client's profile.

## A client's profile

The profile has five tabs: **Overview**, **Orders**, **Profile**, **Access** and
**Timeline**. Each tab loads when you open it.

**Overview:** key facts at a glance and the pinned note. Open positions are coming soon.

**Orders:** the client's orders, by **Open**, **Today** or **Needs attention**.

**Profile:** status, assigned manager and details. With Edit access, **Request manager
change…** asks for a new manager. Identity documents are coming soon.

**Access:** the client's roles and the features they have. Each feature shows the group
default and whether the client has their own setting. You cannot change features here
yet; see Feature rules.

**Timeline:** notes and history, newest first.

## Actions (Edit access)

**Add note:** write a note, and pin it if it should show on the Overview tab.

**Request status change:** choose a new status and give a reason. This is a request.

**Support menu:**

- **Force sign-out:** signs the client out on all devices. You can also clear their local
  data. This applies at once and is recorded.
- **Clear app cache:** the client's app reloads its settings on the next launch. This
  applies at once and is recorded.
- **User features…:** opens the Access tab.
- **Move to group…:** moves the client to another group. This is a request.
- **Open support tickets:** coming soon.
- **Suspend client…:** give a reason. This is a request.

## Sign-ups tab

New sign-ups waiting for a decision. Search by name or email.

**Approve:** needs Approve access. The client is approved at once.

**Decline…:** needs Edit access and a reason.

**Request removal…:** hides the sign-up from view. Nothing is deleted right away, and you
can reverse it later by changing the status back. This is a request.

## Accounts tab

Your clients' accounts, with Client, Type and Status, plus Currency and Broker if you add
them. Filter by Source and Status.

**Request status change…:** with Edit access, choose **Opened** or **Locked** and give a
reason. This is a request.
