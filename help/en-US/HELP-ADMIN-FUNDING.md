# Funding

Funding is hidden until it is enabled for your tenant and the funding ledger is available.
It is meant to record deposits, withdrawals and corrections against client accounts, with
each new operation asked for by one admin and posted by another.

## How to Open

**Menu:** Funding does not appear in the side menu or the drawer until it is enabled for your group. If it is not there, it is not available to you yet.

**Wide screen:** when it is available, choose **Funding** in the side menu on the left.

**Tablet and phone:** below 1000 pixels wide the side menu becomes a drawer. Open it with the menu button at the top left, then choose **Funding**.

## What you see today

**In the menu:** nothing yet. Funding does not appear in the Administration menu.

**If you reach it anyway:** the screen says "Funding is not available for this tenant." It
has no tabs, lists, filters or buttons and shows no client or account data.

**Access:** having the Funding role makes no difference until the ledger is available.
