# Funding

Funding is coming soon. It is planned to bring deposits, withdrawals and transfers across
brokers into one place.

## How to Open

**Wide screen:** choose **Funding** in the side menu on the left. Use the menu button at the top right to collapse the menu to icons or expand it again.

**Tablet and phone:** below 1000 pixels wide the side menu becomes a drawer. Open it with the menu button at the top left, then choose **Funding**.

**Access needed:** You need access to Funding or to transactions. The screen is coming soon, so every level sees the same message.

**Locked:** if you have access in another group but not the one you are working in, **Funding** shows a lock in the menu. Opening it says "You do not have access to Funding in this tenant" and names the group where you do have access. Switch group with **Tenant** at the top. If you have no access in any group, **Funding** is not in the menu at all.

## What you see today

**Title and label:** the screen shows the name **Funding** with the label **Coming soon**.

**Message:** "A cross-broker funding ledger -- deposits, withdrawals and transfers in one
place -- is planned for this tab. Nothing here reads or writes real data yet."

**What you can do:** nothing. There are no tabs, lists, filters or buttons on this screen
yet, and it shows no client or account data.

**Access:** you see this screen if your role includes Funding. Because nothing on it reads
or changes anything, View, Edit and Approve make no difference here today.
